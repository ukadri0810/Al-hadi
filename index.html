01How do I get the price for a Hajj or Umrah package?

Package pricing is shared after discussing your travel date, number of travellers, arrival sector, hotel preference and package level.

02Can I choose my arrival airport or sector?

Yes. For Hajj & Umrah enquiries you can select Madinah, Jeddah, Riyadh or Dammam, or enter another airport or sector manually.

03Can I request a specific hotel?

Yes. Choose Hotel Booking in the enquiry form and enter the hotel name or your preferred stay requirement.

04Can I choose a preferred airline?

Yes. Selecting Flight Tickets reveals an airline list so you can quickly share your preference.

05Which Gulf countries can I enquire about for visa assistance?

The enquiry form includes Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, UAE, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain, plus an option for another Gulf country.

06How do recruitment candidates send their resume?

Fill the candidate form, choose WhatsApp or Email, then attach your resume directly in the app that opens. The website does not store your CV.
