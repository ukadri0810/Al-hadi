IMPORTANT UX NOTES
------------------
1. The Hajj / Umrah and Recruitment experiences are separate modes.
2. Recruitment does NOT upload or store CV files on the website. Candidates fill the short form, choose WhatsApp or Email, and attach their resume in that app before sending.
3. Hajj / Umrah package prices are intentionally not shown. Package CTAs preselect the chosen package in the enquiry form.
4. The address card uses a Google Maps embed for the visual map and the Get Directions button opens the supplied business address in Google Maps.
